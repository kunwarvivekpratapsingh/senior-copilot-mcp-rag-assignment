# Boiler Feed Pump Maintenance Guide

## Routine schedule

| Task | Interval | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Suction strainer inspection | 500 running hours | Shorten if differential rises faster than 0.1 bar/week |
| Bearing lubrication | 2000 running hours | Grease per nameplate; do not over-grease |
| Vibration survey | Monthly | Compare against commissioning baseline |
| Seal inspection | 8000 running hours | Or on evidence of leakage |
| Impeller and wear ring inspection | Overhaul | Or when developed head falls below 90% of curve |

## Interpreting a shortening strainer interval

The interval between strainer cleans is a direct measurement of feedwater solids
loading. If it shortens by more than 30% over three consecutive cycles, the cause is
upstream — raise it with water treatment rather than simply cleaning more often.

## Bearing temperature high

Check lubricant level and condition first; inadequate or degraded lubricant is the
most common cause. Take a vibration reading at the same time: temperature rising
together with vibration indicates mechanical degradation rather than a lubrication
problem, and the two responses are different.

## Wear ring clearance

Developed head falls as wear ring clearance opens. A pump that no longer makes
setpoint discharge pressure with normal suction conditions and a clean strainer has
almost certainly lost clearance. Measure at overhaul and compare against the
as-built value.
